# Scientific-gap status (8 August 2026)

This status distinguishes completion of an analysis from successful removal of a model limitation.

| Scientific issue | Work-package status | Scientific conclusion | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weak rare-ADR retrieval | Completed negative experiment; limitation unresolved | None of the four same-mask 10-fold variants passed the frozen rare-ADR promotion rules. Rare AP was 0.0813 for CAFNet and 0.0782 for fixed CAFNet-DG; the strongest screened learned variant on rare AP was the global gate at 0.0779. Every rare-AP delta versus CAFNet and fixed CAFNet-DG was non-positive and non-significant after Holm correction. | `rare_e2e_screen/` |
| CAFNet-DG is fixed score fusion | Completed negative experiment; limitation unresolved | Global, drug-level, and drug-by-prevalence jointly trained gates did not replace fixed fusion. The best learned-gate aggregate result was the drug-stratum gate (macro AP 0.3999, nDCG@10 0.6159), below fixed CAFNet-DG (0.4134, 0.6262). Routing did not collapse, but stratum-specific gate differences were small. The reported model therefore remains `0.6*CAFNet-D + 0.4*CAFNet`. | `rare_e2e_screen/` |
| No strong independent clinical validation | Partially addressed | Quantitative external validation now covers CT-ADE controlled monopharmacy trial outcomes, OnSIDES v3.1.1 labels, and FDA AEMS/FAERS potential signals. These support limited external prioritization but do not constitute EHR validation, temporal validation, adjudicated causal confirmation, or calibrated incidence prediction. External ordinal calibration on CT-ADE was weak. | `ct_ade_pt_external_validation/`, `ct_ade_frequency_validation/`, `onsides_v311_high_confidence/`, `fda_aems_signal_validation/` |
| Incomplete ordinal-frequency diagnostics | Completed | Cold and warm 10-fold QWK, within-one-class accuracy, exact accuracy, class-wise recall, and 1--5 confusion matrices are available. Cold extreme-class recall remains a measured limitation rather than a missing analysis. | `ordinal_diagnostics/` |
| No complete CAFNet-DG scaffold-disjoint evaluation | Completed | CAFNet, CAFNet-D, and CAFNet-DG were evaluated on all 10 frozen Bemis--Murcko group folds. CAFNet-DG improves overall and non-hot ranking, but rare/middle AP differences are not significant. | `scaffold_cafnet_dg/` |
| No bitwise-determinism guarantee | Audited limitation | Strict deterministic CUDA execution is unsupported by the legacy `torch_scatter` GAT kernel. Three same-seed repeats quantify non-bitwise run-to-run variation. The defensible claim is fixed splits/seeds and statistical reproducibility, not bitwise identity. | `determinism_audit/`, `determinism_strict_status.json`, `determinism_strict_smoke.log` |

The completion audit verifies all six experimental work packages. Verification means that the planned analysis was completed, not that every underlying model limitation was removed. Rare-ADR retrieval, end-to-end fusion, external ordinal calibration, EHR-level validation, and bitwise determinism remain explicit scientific or technical limitations.
